# Crop Research

## Using it
Enter a crop, choose Hydroponic or Soil-based, enter a research category, short note, and website URL. Click SAVE RESEARCH. SAVE TAB URL fills the URL field (on Vercel it can only fill the current website URL). DELETE ALL requires a double-click, as in the Scrimba project.

Research is saved in your browser's localStorage. It is not synced between the Vercel website and Chrome extension or across browsers.

## Four research entries for your assignment
Research and enter at least four genuine entries covering two crops. For example, research lettuce pH, lettuce lighting, tomato watering, and tomato soil nutrients.
